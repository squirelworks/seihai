Notes for the next seihai release. One line per user-visible change, added by the PR that makes it; the release PR moves them into `vX.Y.Z.md` (VERSIONING.md).

- Built on seihai-core `main` (after v0.7.2; was v0.7.0, and before that main at 738a5dd, v0.6.1 plus GS1 `keypressed`). Since v0.7.0: NewWorld COLORS is 8 bytes, the 2.05 and NewWorld file mod time, and GS1 `playeringuild`. From v0.7.0: NewWorld beta 1/2 as real versions, the version-token/profile audit, GS2 reading non-numeric text as -1, and the GS1 fixes from 0.6.2 and 0.6.3.
- 5th Graal to 1.1.9 read `guildname.code` from the player's install folder for GS1 `playeringuild`. The check is an exact, case-sensitive match on the name; no file, or a damaged one, matches no name, as on the real 1.1 and 1.1.9 (lab, 2026-11-20; squirelworks/seihai-core#63).
- Ato (3.153, 4.04r2): a stock Torque `Player` as the control object or camera is read, so its position and facing place the 3D view and the rest of the packet (events, ghosts) is no longer skipped. The layout is the open TGE 1.x one, not yet checked against a real protocol 19 server (#464).
- Ato (Graal3D): `.3ds` and `.dts` shapes are drawn with their textures (PNG, GIF or BMP) once the server has sent them, and the textures are asked for when a shape arrives; faces without one stay flat in their material colour (#461).
- Issho and Ato (GS2 client scripts): every gmap cell the walk leaves keeps its NPC scripts' state, and coming back picks them up where they were; `onPlayerEnters` runs on them only if the cell left the neighbour ring meanwhile. A click runs `onMouseDown(modifier)` on every NPC and weapon, wherever it lands, and `mousex`/`mousey` follow the pointer. A server `triggerclient` (`clientside,<weapon>,…`) runs that weapon's `onActionClientSide`. All match a real 6.03r7 (lab, 2026-11-20; #497).
